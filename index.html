2. Software Description and Purposes of Processing

The software is a desktop application designed to support endurance coaching, workout planning, training management, and athletic performance analysis.

The software may use authorized integrations with Garmin Connect to:

  • Retrieve authorized sports activity data and compatible training files.
  • Analyze athletic performance and training metrics.
  • Plan and transmit structured workouts to compatible accounts and devices.
  • Support training monitoring and the personalization of training programs.

Personal data is processed for the purposes described in this policy and within the scope of the permissions granted.

3. Categories of Personal Data Processed

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted, the software may process the following categories of data:

  • Identification details and information needed to associate an athlete with their account.
  • Sports activity data, including FIT files, duration, distance, speed, heart rate, and power.
  • Information about planned and completed workouts.
  • Physiological and performance metrics made available through authorized integrations.
  • Technical information required for software operation, synchronization, and troubleshooting.

Not all categories of data are necessarily collected for every athlete. Processing depends on the features used and the permissions granted.

4. Data Sources and Sharing

Data may be obtained from connected sports accounts and services, subject to the permissions and authorizations available.

When the Garmin Connect integration is used, data may be received from Garmin or transmitted to Garmin for authorized synchronization and workout delivery operations.

Access to data is limited to coaching purposes and operations necessary for the software to function. Any additional service providers, cloud services, or recipients will be identified according to the integrations actually used.
